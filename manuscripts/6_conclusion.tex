\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

As Large Language Models evolve from isolated chatbots into autonomous agents deeply embedded within enterprise infrastructure, the security paradigm must adapt. This paper introduced the Compositional Attack Path Scoring (CAPS) framework, a novel, graph-theoretic approach to quantifying systemic risk in LLM deployment stacks. By modeling "Effective Exploitability" and introducing an exponential chaining decay factor, CAPS provides a rigorous mathematical foundation that outperforms traditional component-level scoring methods. Our evaluations demonstrate that CAPS significantly improves risk calibration, eliminating the inflation endemic to isolated scoring and providing actionable, ROI-driven mitigation recommendations. Ultimately, CAPS enables organizations to deploy complex AI architectures with confidence, prioritizing defenses where they yield the maximum strategic impact.
